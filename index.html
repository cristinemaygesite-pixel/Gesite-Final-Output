ABOUT OUR SYSTEM

The Bags & Accessories Store E-Commerce Inventory & Search System is a C++ project that demonstrates the use of data structures and algorithms for product inventory management, searching, sorting, shopping cart operations, checkout processing, and brand showcasing.

Our official partner brands are Herschel, Anello, Guess, and Jansport.
