About the Project

Understanding our inventory management system.

The Bags & Accessories Store – E-Commerce Inventory & Search System is a console-based C++ application developed to manage a collection of bags and accessories from different partner brands.


The system provides product management, inventory searching, brand filtering, shopping cart operations, customer checkout, sorting, and undo functionality. It applies different Data Structures and Algorithms to organize and process information.
